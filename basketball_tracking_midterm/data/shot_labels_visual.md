# shot_labels_visual.csv

Shot attempts and outcomes in experiment10/11/12, labelled by eye by Claude on 2026-11-26
from rim close-up frame strips. Candidates were every passage of the ball near the rim in
the ground truth (experiment10) or in the real-time tracker output (experiment11/12), so a
shot that the tracker never brought near the rim could be missing. Frame ranges are
approximate (release to the ball leaving the rim area). Not yet checked by the researcher.
